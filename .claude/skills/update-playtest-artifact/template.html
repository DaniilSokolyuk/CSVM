Testing CSVM — start here

Two kinds of tasks below. Read this once, then jump to whichever tab you're doing.

CSVM is a from-scratch remake of Crimson Skies (2000), built to play using your own legally-owned game files. We're checking it against the original — that's where you come in.

CAP — film the original. Each row under Owed Captures asks for a short clip or screenshot of the original Crimson Skies — the "Unblocks" column just tells us which of our own questions it settles.

⚠ These captures record what something looks and sounds like. Film the whole event with a steady, unobstructed view of it — we read the footage, we don't measure numbers off it.

PT — fly our remake. Each item under Actionable Flights asks you to fly a specific setup in CSVM and judge specific things ("Look for"). Every section shows how to get into that flight; click an item to see the full checklist.

Found something, good or bad? Send footage/notes to [wherever you're collecting this from testers].
